A student-led fundraiser

Strum a song,
fund a pad kit, heal a paw.

We are students who play guitar concerts and sell donated guitars to fund two things that too often go unfunded: sanitary pad kits for communities where pads aren't government supplied, and vet care for injured street dogs and rescued animals.

Two causes, one fund

Every rupee is split by the cause you pick. If you don't pick one, we divide it equally.

Cause 1

Menstrual hygiene access

In many communities pads are not provided by the government, and families choose between pads and food. Missed school days follow. We buy and hand out sanitary pad kits with local partners.

One kit
$5
Inside
Pads for three cycles, disposal bags, a cloth pouch and an illustrated care card

Cause 2

Injured & rescued animals

Street dogs hit by traffic and animals rescued from neglect need a vet, medicine and time to heal. We pay partner clinics directly for treatment, so the money goes to care rather than to us.

One treatment
About $40
Covers
Examination, wound dressing or X-ray, medicines and a follow-up visit

Guitars for a Cause

Buy a guitar, fund both causes

People donate guitars they no longer play. We clean them, restring them, set them up and sell them. 100% of every sale is split equally between pad kits and animal vet care.

Concerts for a Cause

Every few months our student musicians put on a live acoustic concert, often playing the donated guitars that are up for sale. Ticket money, on-the-spot donations and guitar sales on the night all go into the fund.
